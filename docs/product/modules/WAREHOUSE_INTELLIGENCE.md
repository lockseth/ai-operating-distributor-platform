# Module Spec — Warehouse Intelligence (Roadmap)

## Purpose

Warehouse Intelligence menjadi pengembangan lanjutan untuk mengontrol stok, picking, loading, receiving, dan kehilangan barang.

## Roadmap Features

- Stock movement
- Picking checklist
- Loading verification
- Delivery quantity confirmation
- Barcode / QR scan
- Photo proof
- FIFO monitoring
- Missing stock detection
- Multi warehouse
- Driver/delivery proof

## Future AI Jobs

- detectQuantityMismatch
- detectStockLeakage
- summarizeWarehouseRisk
- recommendAuditAction

## Important Case

Jika PO 300 dus dan pabrik/gudang mengirim 300 dus, tetapi customer hanya menerima 150 dus, sistem harus bisa membantu melacak titik kehilangan:

1. PO
2. Picking
3. Loading
4. Delivery
5. Customer receiving
